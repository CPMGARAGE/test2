WELCOME TO TERMINAL TANKS!

    
Turn-based tank artillery, drawn entirely in a terminal. Set your angle, set your power, read the wind, and fire.

    
Each turn the wind changes and the ground you stand on is part of the weapon: shells carve craters, and a blast beside a tank still counts. You can play it here with nothing to install, on a keyboard, a touchscreen or a gamepad.
